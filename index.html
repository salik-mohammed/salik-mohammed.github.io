AI and LLM work

Since 2020 I have worked as an independent contractor across AI training, search evaluation and language data projects. The clients and project names stay confidential, so what follows is the kind of work rather than who it was for.

Model response evaluation

  • Judge model responses against detailed quality rubrics: accuracy, helpfulness, instruction-following, tone and safety.
  • Compare two or more outputs side by side and pick the stronger one, with a written justification for the choice.
  • Write rationales that explain why a response passes or fails a guideline. These get used to calibrate other reviewers.
  • Flag hallucinations, unsupported claims, harmful content and guideline violations.

Prompt engineering

  • Write prompts across a wide range of tasks and difficulty levels to test and stretch model capability.
  • Build adversarial and edge-case prompts that surface weaknesses in reasoning, factuality and instruction-following.
  • Refine wording, structure and constraints until output becomes consistent.
  • Author ideal reference responses that serve as training and grading targets.

Search and information retrieval

  • Rate search results and ranked result sets against relevance and quality guidelines.
  • Evaluate advertisement relevance, landing page quality and policy compliance.
  • Read ambiguous user intent and apply long written guidelines the same way every time.

Linguistic and multilingual data

  • Annotate, label and classify text for machine learning training sets.
  • Transcribe, localize and quality-check audio and text in English, Hindi, Urdu and Marathi.
  • Review localized content for fluency, cultural fit and terminology accuracy.
  • Run quality assurance on annotated datasets and fix labelling inconsistencies.
